# Juni

> Juni is a Swedish fintech (Göteborg, founded 2020) offering business banking, spend management, multi-currency accounts, corporate/virtual cards, financing, and AI-driven accounting for ecommerce and digital businesses. Licensed under the Swedish FSA (SFSA) and backed by EQT Ventures. Juni exposes a regulatory PSD2 / Open Banking (ASPSP) API surface but publishes no public product API, SDKs, or developer portal.
